When the judge doesn't rescue, both sides genuinely believe they won

5 runs hit a mechanical mismatch after both capitals accepted; 3 were the same package described differently. The 2 that weren't are the clearest illustration of the unitary-actor problem in the whole set.

control rep1 — each side ratified a different number and called it convergence

EU's final position: 900,000t pool / 800,000t tranche. UK's final position: 1,050,000t / 900,000t. The judge's own words:

"Both sides' internal acceptance rationales characterize the outcome as 'full convergence'... but neither rationale documents the other side actually agreeing to its counterpart's tranche/pool figure."

control rep4 — each side believes it won the other's opening offer

EU's rationale describes accepting a 650,000t UK tranche at 25%→18%. UK's rationale describes accepting 480,000t at 27%→19%. The judge again:

"Each side believes it 'won' the other side's opening/closing figures... indicating the two delegations closed on mutually exclusive readings of what was agreed rather than a single converged package."

Other findings worth flagging

The 4-round deadline is doing the work, not organic convergence

All 10 settlements land at exactly round 4 - none earlier. Combined with the earlier rounds comparison (6-round baseline settling 3/4 vs this set's 62.5%), the deadline itself looks like a major lever on the outcome, separate from disposition. Worth deliberately checking round-by-round proposal gaps in the non-final rounds to see whether real movement happens throughout, or whether rounds 1-3 are largely positioning before a round-4 crunch.

package_incoherent's two occurrences share more than chance would suggest

Both instances in this set - firm rep4 and focal_firm_ukgva rep4 - are round 1, seat uk-geneva, first occurrence. Same rep number, same seat, same round, across two different arms. n=2 is too thin to claim a disposition link; worth checking whether something about that particular batch (timing, a shared upstream condition) rather than the arm explains it.

n=4 per arm - same caveat as every set so far

The settlement-rate staircase is clean enough to be a real signal, not noise - a monotonic 4/4 order across 4 independent categories is a 1-in-24 arrangement if the true rates were tied. The specific numbers (100/75/50/25%) and the settled-terms figures should still be read as directional, not precise, until the sample grows.
