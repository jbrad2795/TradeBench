Without prejudging the outcome, the EU opens with a global tariff-rate quota of 1,200,000 tonnes annually, administered on a first-come, first-served basis, with a 10% out-of-quota bound rate. We also invite discussion of the treatment of EU exporters under the UK's steel safeguard measure, on the understanding that all elements form part of a balanced package. This is an opening position, and we look forward to hearing the UK's priorities and evidence on trade flows and quota administration.
