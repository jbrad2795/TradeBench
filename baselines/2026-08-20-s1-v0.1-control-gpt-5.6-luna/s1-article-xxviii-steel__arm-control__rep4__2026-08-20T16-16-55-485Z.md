### EU / Geneva

**Objectives, most important first**

1. Conduct a procedurally sound Article XXVIII negotiation while preserving the EU's ability to implement the notified modification.
1. Secure a settlement that materially reduces disruption to legitimate UK steel trade without conceding more quota volume, administration, duration or compensation than necessary.
1. Maintain the EU's flexibility to address global overcapacity and avoid creating an undesirable precedent for the parallel claims of Turkey, Korea, India and Japan.
1. Seek reciprocal, workable treatment for EU steel exports under the UK's own safeguard measure.
1. Protect the EU's internal balance between steel-producing member states and steel-consuming industries, while keeping the outcome administrable and WTO-defensible.
